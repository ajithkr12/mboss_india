Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about MBOSS Lexon LLP's services, location and approach.

What legal services does MBOSS Lexon LLP provide? +

MBOSS Lexon LLP provides corporate law, banking and finance advisory, commercial contracts, startup advisory, compliance, intellectual property, litigation and business legal solutions. The firm serves corporates, startups, banks, NBFCs, MSMEs and financial institutions across Kerala and India.

Where is MBOSS Lexon LLP located? +

MBOSS Lexon LLP is registered in Kerala, India and provides legal services across Kerala and beyond. The firm serves clients in Kochi, Trivandrum, Kozhikode, Thrissur and other cities across Kerala, as well as clients across India.

Does MBOSS Lexon LLP provide startup legal services? +

Yes. The firm supports startups with incorporation, LLP/company structuring, DPIIT recognition, founder agreements, fundraising documentation, IP protection and compliance. MBOSS Lexon LLP also advises startups seeking KSUM (Kerala Startup Mission) support and investor readiness.

Can MBOSS Lexon LLP help banks and NBFCs? +

Yes. The firm provides loan documentation, security creation, recovery proceedings, SARFAESI advisory, RBI compliance and financial legal services. MBOSS Lexon LLP has dedicated expertise in banking and finance law, supporting both scheduled banks and non-banking financial companies.

Does MBOSS Lexon LLP handle corporate contracts? +

Yes. The firm drafts and reviews commercial agreements including MoUs, NDAs, vendor agreements, service agreements and business contracts. Every contract is tailored to protect client interests and minimise commercial risk.

Does MBOSS Lexon LLP provide legal consultation online? +

Yes. The firm provides technology-enabled consultation and communication facilities for clients. This includes online meetings, digital document delivery and remote advisory, making it convenient for businesses across India to access quality legal services.

What makes MBOSS Lexon LLP different from traditional law firms? +

MBOSS Lexon LLP combines legal expertise with technology-driven processes to provide faster, transparent and business-focused solutions. Unlike traditional firms, the practice is structured around modern workflows, structured client communication, document automation and measurable outcomes.
