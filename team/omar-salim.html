Profile

Adv. Omar Salim

Founding Partner, CEO

Hailing from Kayamkulam in Alappuzha District, in Kerala, Adv. Omar Salim developed a deep interest and passion in law and social service right from his childhood. Inspired by the exemplary career and values put forth by his father, Adv. Mohammed Basheer, he was naturally drawn to the legal profession.

After completing his pre degree at MSM College, Kayamkulam, he pursued his legal education at Visveswarapura College of Law, Bangalore. Thereafter, he embarked on his legal career and has then relentlessly worked hard and established himself as a distinguished legal practitioner.

Renowned for his impeccable advocacy, strategic cross-examinations, and meticulous drafting skills, he has earned the trust of his clients and respect of his fellow beings. For a travel enthusiast like him, the representation of cases across the country is definitely a feather in his cap.

His areas of practice include arbitration, civil law, family law, criminal law, company law, labour disputes, and other allied legal matters. With over 19+ years of dedicated experience in the field of litigation, he has built a distinguished reputation of integrity, and excellence, carving a unique identity within the legal fraternity.
