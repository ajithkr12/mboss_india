FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an NDA and when do I need one?+

A Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) protects confidential business information shared with employees, contractors, investors, or business partners. You need an NDA before sharing any proprietary information, trade secrets, business plans or technical know-how. MBOSS Lexon LLP drafts NDAs tailored to your specific disclosure situation.

What is a vendor agreement?+

A vendor agreement defines the terms of engagement between a business and its supplier or service provider — including scope, payment, delivery, warranties, liability and termination. A well-drafted vendor agreement prevents disputes and protects both parties.

Can MBOSS Lexon LLP review contracts drafted by other parties?+

Yes. Contract review is a core service. We review contracts presented by counterparties and identify clauses that may be unfavourable, missing or unenforceable, and advise on negotiations or amendments to protect your interests.
