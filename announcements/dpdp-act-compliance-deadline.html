DPDP Act Compliance Deadline — What Businesses Need to Know

Legal Update MBOSS Lexon LLP June 28, 2026
🔒
MBOSS LEXON LLP · LEGAL UPDATE

As enforcement of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) progresses, businesses across sectors need to reassess their data handling practices. Our team has been actively advising clients on compliance readiness ahead of key regulatory timelines.

Key Obligations Businesses Should Not Ignore

  • Establishing a valid legal basis and consent mechanism for processing personal data
  • Appointing a Data Protection Officer where applicable
  • Implementing reasonable security safeguards against data breaches
  • Setting up grievance redressal mechanisms for data principals
  • Maintaining records of processing activities and consent artifacts

Practical Steps We Recommend

Businesses should begin by mapping what personal data they collect, why, and how long it is retained. From there, consent notices, privacy policies and internal data handling processes should be reviewed and updated to align with DPDP Act requirements.
