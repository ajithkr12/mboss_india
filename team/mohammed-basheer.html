Profile

Adv. Mohammed Basheer

Founding Partner, CEO

Hard work and dedication have always been the very essence of Adv. Mohammed Basheer's distinguished legal career. He embraced the noble profession of law with unwavering passion, integrity, and commitment.

After completing his degree he pursued his LL.B. at Government Law College, Thiruvananthapuram, affiliated with the University of Kerala.

A voracious reader with an unquenchable thirst for knowledge, Adv. Mohammed Basheer has continually sharpened his skills as an advocate through relentless effort, perseverance, and lifelong learning. He has always been meticulous in keeping himself updated on the latest judicial pronouncements and legislative developments. Not a single day passes without him beginning his morning with The Hindu, reflecting his steadfast commitment to remaining informed and updated. Beyond his legal acumen, he is widely regarded as a mentor par excellence, serving as a source of guidance, inspiration, and encouragement to generations of junior advocates and fellow members of the Bar.

With nearly five decades of distinguished practice at the Bar, Adv. Mohammed Basheer has earned an enviable reputation for his exceptional proficiency in both civil and criminal litigation. His profound legal knowledge, unwavering integrity, and dedication to the cause of justice have earned him the trust of his clients, the respect and admiration of his colleagues, and the esteem of the Bench. Throughout his career, he has consistently upheld the highest standards of the legal profession, establishing a legacy of excellence, integrity, and dedicated service.
