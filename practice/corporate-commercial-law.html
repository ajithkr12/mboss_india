FAQ

Corporate Law FAQs

What is the difference between a Private Limited Company and an LLP?+

A Private Limited Company is governed by the Companies Act 2013 and is suitable for businesses seeking venture funding. An LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) is governed by the LLP Act 2008, offers flexible profit-sharing, lower compliance costs and is preferred for professional services and small businesses. MBOSS Lexon LLP advises on the optimal structure based on your specific business needs.

What corporate governance documents does a company need?+

Key corporate governance documents include Memorandum of Association, Articles of Association, Shareholders Agreement, Board Resolutions, Appointment Letters for Directors, Statutory Registers and Annual Compliance filings. MBOSS Lexon LLP assists in drafting, reviewing and maintaining all these documents.

Does MBOSS Lexon LLP handle M&A transactions?+

Yes. MBOSS Lexon LLP advises on mergers, acquisitions, slump sales, asset transfers and business restructuring. Services include due diligence, transaction structuring, SPA/BTA drafting and regulatory filings.

What is corporate law compliance in India?+

Corporate compliance includes annual ROC filings, board meeting requirements, maintenance of statutory registers, director KYC, AGM compliance and various event-based filings under the Companies Act. MBOSS Lexon LLP provides annual compliance retainer services to manage all these requirements.
