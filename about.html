About MBOSS Lexon LLP

A technology-enabled legal services firm built for the modern business environment — precise, practical and always on.

Our Story

Building a New Kind of Law Firm

MBOSS Lexon LLP was established with a clear purpose: to create a law firm that truly serves the needs of modern businesses. Registered as a Limited Liability Partnership in Kerala, India, the firm was built from the ground up with a technology-first mindset and a deep commitment to client outcomes.

Kerala's business landscape is evolving rapidly — startups are emerging, MSMEs are expanding, financial institutions are growing, and international trade is increasing. We recognised that businesses in this environment need legal partners who are not just legally proficient but also commercially aware and technologically equipped.

From our base in Kerala, we serve clients across the state and beyond — providing the kind of sophisticated, business-aware legal advice that was previously available only to larger corporations in metro cities.
