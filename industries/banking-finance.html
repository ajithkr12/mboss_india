FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SARFAESI and when can a bank use it?+

The SARFAESI Act 2002 allows banks and qualified financial institutions to enforce their security interest without court intervention when a borrower's account is classified as NPA. The lender issues a demand notice under Section 13(2), and if unpaid within 60 days, can take possession and sell the secured assets. MBOSS Lexon LLP manages all aspects of SARFAESI enforcement for financial institutions.

What documentation is needed for a home loan in Kerala?+

Home loan documentation in Kerala includes: loan agreement, promissory note, Mortgage Deed (Simple or MODT), property documents with original title deeds, encumbrance certificate, approved building plan, possession certificate, and insurance assignment. MBOSS Lexon LLP drafts and verifies all home loan security documentation.

Does MBOSS Lexon LLP advise NBFCs on RBI compliance?+

Yes. MBOSS Lexon LLP advises NBFCs on RBI Master Directions compliance, Fair Practice Code requirements, KYC compliance, interest rate policy, recovery practices, digital lending guidelines and annual regulatory filings. We also assist NBFCs with application for NBFC registration and changes in business activities.

What is DRT and how does it work?+

The Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT) is a specialised tribunal for recovery of debts above INR 20 lakh by banks and financial institutions. MBOSS Lexon LLP files and manages Original Applications (OAs) before DRT, including application, hearings, interim orders, Recovery Certificates and Recovery Officer proceedings.
