Internship Programme

Bridging the gap between legal education and practical advocacy.

At MBOSS Lexon LLP, we strongly believe that the study of law is complete only when it extends beyond the classroom into the courtroom. Our Internship Programme is thoughtfully curated to provide law students with meaningful practical exposure through observation of proceedings before various courts, tribunals and forums across Kerala, while also offering opportunities to develop their legal drafting and research skills.

We invite applications from students pursuing both Three-Year and Five-Year LL.B. programmes who are eager to learn, observe, and enhance their professional competence.

What We Offer

Practical Training, Real Exposure

Court & Tribunal Observation

Opportunity to observe proceedings before various courts, tribunals and forums.

Litigation Exposure

Practical exposure to litigation and client handling.

Drafting & Research

Training in the fundamentals of legal drafting, pleadings, and research.

Mentorship

Interaction and guidance from experienced advocates.

Professional Grounding

Insight into professional ethics, courtroom etiquette, and case preparation.

📝

Bonus Opportunity

Upon successful completion of the Internship Programme, interns will have the opportunity to publish their research articles and legal papers on our website on a rolling basis, subject to editorial review and approval. This initiative aims to encourage legal research and academic writing.

🎓

Certificate of Internship

Upon successful completion of the Internship Programme, each candidate will be awarded a Certificate of Internship acknowledging their participation and valuable contribution.

We look forward to mentoring aspiring legal professionals and helping them bridge the gap between legal education and practical advocacy.
