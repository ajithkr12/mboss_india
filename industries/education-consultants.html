FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What registrations does an education consultancy need in Kerala?+

Education consultancies in Kerala typically need: entity registration (company/LLP), GST registration, Shops & Establishments registration, and for overseas education recruitment — FEMA registration where applicable. MBOSS Lexon LLP advises on the complete registration requirements.

What should a student recruitment agreement cover?+

A student recruitment agreement should clearly cover: services provided, fees and payment terms, refund policy, timelines, counsellor obligations, data handling, dispute resolution and jurisdiction. Properly drafted agreements prevent disputes and protect the consultancy from liability.

How does FEMA apply to education consultancies?+

Education consultancies receiving payments from foreign institutions or making remittances abroad must comply with FEMA. This includes maintaining proper documentation, following RBI guidelines on current account transactions, and complying with anti-money laundering requirements.
