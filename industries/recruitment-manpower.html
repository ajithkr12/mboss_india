Sector Overview

Dedicated Legal Expertise for Recruitment & Manpower Companies

Kerala is a major hub for international labour migration, and the state has a large recruitment and manpower industry supporting workers going to the Gulf, Europe, and other destinations. MBOSS Lexon LLP provides comprehensive legal support to licensed and unlicensed recruitment agencies operating in Kerala.

The recruitment industry is heavily regulated — by the Emigration Act, MEA, state licensing requirements, and bilateral agreements. Non-compliance can result in license suspension, criminal proceedings and reputational damage. Our legal team helps recruitment firms navigate this complex landscape.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Recruiting Agent (RA) licence?+

A Recruiting Agent licence is issued by the Protector General of Emigrants under the Emigration Act 1983. It authorises a company to recruit Indian workers for overseas employment. MBOSS Lexon LLP advises on RA licence applications, renewals and compliance requirements.

What should a recruitment agreement with an overseas employer include?+

A recruitment agreement with an overseas employer should cover: recruitment fees, candidate specifications, validity, replacement obligations, visa sponsorship, wage protection, dispute resolution and jurisdiction. MBOSS Lexon LLP drafts and reviews these agreements to protect the Indian recruitment agency.

What are the legal risks for recruitment agencies in Kerala?+

Key risks include: unlicensed recruitment (criminal offence), visa fraud exposure, worker exploitation claims, demand letter fraud, disputes with overseas employers, and labour law violations. MBOSS Lexon LLP helps agencies identify and mitigate these risks through proper legal frameworks.
